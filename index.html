01 · Enterprise and marketplaces · Platform engineering

A platform that puts enterprise projects in front of senior engineers

Client
AltCampus, a freelance-style platform where large enterprises, including banks, send projects to senior engineers
Services
Product engineering, Web application

The situation

Large enterprises, including banks, wanted experienced engineers for project work without a long hiring cycle. AltCampus runs the platform where those projects are posted and picked up by senior engineers with more than 15 years of experience. A marketplace like this lives or dies on trust: both sides need to know the other is serious.

What we built

Our founder worked on the platform's engineering, building the product that enterprise clients and senior engineers use day to day.

What the client needed

  • A clear way for an enterprise to describe a project and send it to the right engineers
  • A smaller, vetted pool of senior engineers instead of an open marketplace
  • Handling of confidential project information, which matters most to banks
  • A simple workflow from a posted project to accepted work
  • Two audiences with different needs: enterprise buyers and senior engineers

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask us directly

How much does a project cost?

Every project is scoped and quoted in writing after a short discovery call. Landing pages and small sites sit at the low end; custom web apps and AI systems depend on scope. Share your budget range on the contact form and we will tell you honestly what fits.

How long does a website take?

A focused marketing site usually ships in two to four weeks. Larger web apps and AI automation projects are planned in phases so something useful goes live early.

Can you work with teams in the US, UK or Gulf?

Yes. We work remotely from India and keep overlap hours with your time zone for calls and weekly demos.

Do you handle hosting, SEO and ads after launch?

Yes. We can host, maintain and improve your site, set up search and paid campaigns, and report on leads each month.

Can I talk to you on WhatsApp?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp any time during our working hours and we will reply the same day.

Tell us what you are trying to build.

Our founder reads every message and replies personally, not a salesperson. A few lines about your project are enough.
