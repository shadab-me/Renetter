Why your site gets visits but no enquiries

By the Renetter team4 min read

Traffic without enquiries is frustrating, and it is usually fixable. The cause is rarely one big thing. It is a handful of small friction points that add up.

The page does not say what you do

A visitor should understand what you offer, who it is for and what to do next within a few seconds. If the headline is a slogan, replace it with a plain sentence.

The site is slow or awkward on a phone

Most first visits happen on a phone. Open your own site on mobile data. If text is small, buttons are hard to tap or the page jumps while loading, visitors leave.

There is no obvious next step

One clear button, repeated in the right places, beats five competing links. Make the main action visible without scrolling and again at the end of the page.

The form asks for too much

Every extra field costs you some people. Ask only for what you need to reply, and tell people what happens after they submit.

Nothing builds trust

Show real names, real work and a way to reach a person. A visible phone number or WhatsApp link reassures visitors more than a long list of claims.

Fix these in order: message, speed, next step, form, trust. Then test one change at a time so you know what worked.
